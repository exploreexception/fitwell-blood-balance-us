Limitations & Risks

One of the major downsides to FitWell Blood Balance is that the evidence for the finished formula may not be as broad as the evidence for the individual ingredients. Even when a particular nutrient has been studied in clinical research, the results may involve a different dose, extract, formulation or population. It would be inappropriate to assume that all of the effects claimed have been demonstrated for the specific product.

There are also important safety factors. Ingredients that affect glucose metabolism could, in theory, cause blood sugar to decline below a healthy level when combined with diabetes medications or other glucose-lowering methods. Some botanical ingredients may also influence the effects of medications or cause digestive symptoms. If you have a chronic medical condition, are pregnant or nursing, or are taking prescription medication, discuss use with your health care provider before using a new formula.

More than anything, the use of supplements should not be a way to delay a medical assessment. Also, if you are unusually thirsty, urinate often, lose or gain weight without an explanation, have high or low blood pressure readings, have dizziness, have chest pain, or other troubling symptoms, you need qualified medical assistance, not self-treatment with a blood-balance product.

Alternative Approaches
